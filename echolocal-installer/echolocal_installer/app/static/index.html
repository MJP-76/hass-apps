Before you start: the Dot must already be unlocked with TWRP (the amonet-biscuit unlock) and connected to this host with a data USB cable. Boot it into TWRP recovery first: with power unplugged, hold ONLY the Volume Up button, plug power in and keep holding until the LED ring is steady white — release once recovery is up (Volume Down = fastboot, Mute = USBDL, not TWRP). This is a manual step the add-on cannot trigger. Leave recovery mode alone after the flash; echoctl reboots the device itself.
Help: from a PC, amonet's fastboot-step.sh (kept in this repo — known to work) flashes TWRP and boots recovery from hacked fastboot (Volume Down) · installing EchoLocal replaces the Dot's Amazon OS.
